# Endings

## Prefinal endings [#prefinal]

EP marks honorific `시`, past `었/았`, retrospective `더`, and conjectural `겠` before final endings. `먹었겠지만` is `먹/VV+었/EP+겠/EP+지만/EC`.

A transition table bounds their order. Repeated EP does not permit arbitrary permutations.

`v:먹다` matches `먹었겠지만`, where past precedes conjectural, but not the reversed `먹겠었지만`, which has no generated transition path.

## Final endings [#final]

EF terminates, EC connects, ETN nominalizes, and ETM adnominalizes. `먹는다`, `먹으면`, `먹기`, and `먹는` close in different final states.

After a final ending closes the chain, only permitted particles or punctuation boundaries may follow. Ending concatenation is bounded.

## Ending chains [#chains]

The generator separates stem alternation from ending transitions and then assembles surface forms. Identical surface forms from several grammar paths merge their origins.

A plausible chain absent from rule data is not generated. Coverage additions include both fixtures and rule IDs.
